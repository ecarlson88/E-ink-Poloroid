Two cards: the compact case (reset through a pinhole) and the acrylic-front case (button on the bottom edge). Print, then keep the one for the magnet you built. Replace "Erik" in the last line if someone else should be called.

Your photo magnet

A new photo every day, all by itself.

Every day

The magnet changes its photo once a day. It needs your home Wi-Fi to get new photos. It holds the picture even with no power.

Charging

When a small battery sign appears in a corner of the photo, charge it with a USB-C phone charger. It lasts months between charges.

New Wi-Fi or new password?

If the magnet can't connect, it shows a Wi-Fi setup screen by itself. To start setup yourself: push a paperclip gently into the small hole on the back, twice (the second press within 5 seconds).

  1. On your phone, open Wi-Fi settings and join FridgeMagnet-Setup.
  2. A page opens. (If not, open any website.)
  3. Choose your home Wi-Fi, type its password, tap Save.

A new photo appears in about a minute. Pressing the hole once just shows a new photo.

Questions? Call Erik. Please don't open the case.
